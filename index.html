To analyze the land use and green cover within a 5 km × 5 km square area using satellite imagery and AI-based image analysis, identify features such as gardens, colleges, residential areas, malls, shops and parking areas, and determine locations where tree plantation is required due to insufficient green cover.

        How to read the numbers

        Every cell has a green cover value between 0% and 100%, the share of that cell covered by trees or vegetation. Area figures add these up. In the sample area the values are generated along with the picture. In an uploaded image they are measured as the share of pixels whose excess green index, 2G − R − B on normalised colours, is above the sensitivity setting.

        The sample area is example data and does not describe a real place. Real-map imagery is © Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics and the GIS User Community; place search is by OpenStreetMap Nominatim. Land use and object counts come from OpenStreetMap, which volunteers map, so some features may be missing. Use the results for study and check sites on the ground.
